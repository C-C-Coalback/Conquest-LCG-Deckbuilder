question: A Furious Wraithblade is destroyed. Gift of Isha puts the unit back into play. Can the unit trigger their ability again?
related: Furious_Wraithblade
---
Yes. The Furious Wraithblade that has been put into play is a "new instance" of the unit, and therefore has all attributes associated with the card, such as temporary ATK or HP bonuses, as well as limits on their abilities, reset.
