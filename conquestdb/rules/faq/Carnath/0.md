question: Carnath is used to trigger Atrox Prime. Do we target a planet adjacent to Carnath or Atrox Prime?
related: Atrox_Prime
---
You can target a planet that is adjacent to Atrox Prime. You cannot target a planet that is adjacent to Carnath.

Carnath is triggering the battle ability of Atrox Prime, and is not copying it. Therefore you use Atrox Prime's ability from the current location of Atrox Prime, and not Carnath's location.
