question: Can Clogged with Corpses destroy a 0-cost support with sacrificing any Termagant units?
related: 
---
No. If not Termagant units are sacrificed, then the additional cost of the card, i.e. to sacrifice X Termagants, has not been paid, as no Termagants were sacrificed. You will need to sacrifice at least one Termagant to destroy a 0-cost support.
