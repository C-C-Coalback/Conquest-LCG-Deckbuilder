question: Does Sacaellum Infestors consider the additional card and resource bonus from Genestealer Harvesters?
related: Genestealer_Harvesters, Toxic_Venomthrope
---
No. The additional card and resource bonus from Genestealer Harvesters only applies when a command struggle is won at that planet. This still does not apply even if a Toxic Venomthrope is responsible for the infestation, as this occurs after the command struggle is won, not during the command struggle.
