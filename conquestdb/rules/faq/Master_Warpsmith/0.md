question: An Elite is being deployed, and a Cultist is sacrificed. Does Master Warpsmith apply the discount to that Elite or the next Elite?
related: Cultist
---
The Master Warpsmith applies the discount to the Elite currently being deployed.

"If you are sacrificing a Cultist to reduce the cost of an Elite Daemon, that will be the "next Elite" you deploy that phase.  This is because the reactions to sacrificing each Cultist would be nested within the interrupt window. So the reaction to Cultist #1 is actually resolved before Cultist #2 is sacrificed, and reactions to Cultist #2 are resolved before the Interrupt window to deploying the Elite Daemon closes.

Remember that all of this is happening in the "modify cost" step for deploying that card. You haven't even paid the cost for the Daemon yet, let alone actually deployed it.

So, since the reactions resolve before you have actually deployed your Elite Daemon, that unit is the "next Elite unit you deploy this phase," and the cost reduction will apply to it.

If the Daemon you sacrifice the Cultists for is not an Elite, the two "reduce by 1" effects will still be pending for another Elite unit you deploy that phase - Daemon or not."

- ktom, "Master Warpsmith - What is Next Elite?", archived from cardgamedb
