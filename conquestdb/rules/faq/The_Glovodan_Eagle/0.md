question: Mind Shackle Scarab takes control of The Glovodan Eagle. The unit's Action is then triggered. Does the unit go to the Necrons player's hand?
related: Mind_Shackle_Scarab
---
No. Cards which are returned to an out-of-play area are always returned to their owner's out-of-play area. The Glovodan Eagle would be returned to the Astra Militarum player's hand.
